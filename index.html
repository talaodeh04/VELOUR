Our Story

Made to be worn, not just bought.

VELOUR started as a question: why does most beauty and accessory packaging look disposable? We work with small studios and family-run ateliers who care as much about how a piece ages as how it looks on day one.

Every formula is tested for wear across a full day, and every bag is built on a frame meant to outlast a season. We'd rather sell fewer things, well, than fill a catalog.

Thank you for shopping with us — we hope these pieces earn a permanent spot in your routine.
